Panda Unexpress

Run a bubble-tea shop with your own two hands, in VR.

team
6 · Apr–Jun 2026
engine
Unity · Meta Quest
role
Gameplay & systems

Customers walk in with orders and limited patience. Grab a cup, scoop ice, pull the spigot to pour tea, add sugar, toppings and boba, seal it and hand it over. Includes voice commands, upgrades, achievements and a guided tutorial.

the fun part Every station is physical. To pour, you pull the spigot handle past 45° and it springs back when you let go. Each cup keeps filling with whatever you pour until it is sealed.

my part The whole drink-making station (cups, spigot, sugar machine, ice scoop, toppings, boba, sealer, recipes), the economy, achievement, event and profile managers, voice commands, hand animation and the game-over screen.
